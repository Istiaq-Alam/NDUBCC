Annual Picnic-2025

Both the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) & the Department of Microbiology at Notre Dame University Bangladesh (NDUB) organized its annual picnic on 25th February 2025 at Chuti Resort. The event was convened by Afsana Sharmin Shanta, lecturer, with other faculty members and students of both departments who worked together to make the event graceful for all the participants. To add a special touch, custom T-shirts were arranged for the participants, making the day event more colorful.
