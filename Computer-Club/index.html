NDUBCC

Objective

NDUBCC was officially inaugurated at 12.45 pm on March 6, 2017. The inauguration also included a prize giving ceremony for the winners from a programming contest held on the same day.

Since its inception, the club has already organized a workshop on competitive programming with the association of SWAP (Student Welfare Association and Platform) and sent 3 teams to BUET RoboTutor v3.0. NDUBCC currently has approximately 180 members and is rapidly growing.
