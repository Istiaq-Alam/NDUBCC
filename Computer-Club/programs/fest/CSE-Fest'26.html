About CSE FEST 2026

The Ultimate Tech Celebration

CSE FEST 2026 is the flagship event organized by NDUB Computer Club, bringing together students, tech enthusiasts, and industry professionals for a three-day celebration of technology, innovation, and competition.

This year's fest promises to be bigger and better with a diverse range of events designed to challenge participants across multiple domains - from competitive programming to gaming tournaments, and from project showcases to tech talks.

  • Multiple competitive events with exciting prizes
  • Opportunities to network with industry professionals
  • Learn from expert speakers and mentors
  • Showcase your projects and innovations
  • Experience the latest in technology and gaming
