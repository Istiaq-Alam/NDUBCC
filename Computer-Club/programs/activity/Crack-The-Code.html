🤝 NDUBCC x St. Joseph IT Club Collaboration

On September 18, 2025, Notre Dame University Bangladesh Computer Club (NDUBCC) collaborated with St. Joseph IT Club for host a Olympiad & Programming Contest on Artificial Intelligence, Competitive Programming.

The Josephite IT Club (JITC) successfully hosted Technovation’25 from 18–20 September at St. Joseph Higher Secondary School, Mohammadpur, Dhaka. Students from over 50 institutions actively participated in nearly thirty events, showcasing their talent and enthusiasm across a wide range of competitions and activities.

We are proud to share that the Notre Dame University Bangladesh Computer Club (NDUBCC) collaborated with JITC in organizing two major events at the festival:

  • AI & Robotics Olympiad (18 September 2025) NDUBCC contributed to the successful hosting of this Olympiad, held across three categories, where students demonstrated their knowledge in the exciting fields of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics.
  • Crack the Code – Programming Contest (19 September 2025) Organized by NDUBCC, this contest drew around 35 participants from more than 25 institutions. Starting at 11:00 AM, the contest ran for 1 hour and 40 minutes, testing participants’ problem-solving and coding abilities in a competitive setting. Despite some technical hurdles, the event concluded successfully.

    • After the contest, NDUBCC members engaged with students, gathered feedback, and encouraged them to take part in more programming competitions. This interaction fostered a collaborative environment and motivated participants to keep honing their skills.
    We are grateful to JITC for the opportunity to collaborate and contribute to the success of Technovation’25. Together, we hope to continue inspiring the next generation of coders, innovators, and tech enthusiasts!
