About The Seminar

This seminar focuses on understanding complex engineering problems and complex engineering activities within professional engineering practice. Participants will explore real-world decision-making, professional responsibility, risk assessment, and advanced problem-solving frameworks used in modern engineering.

A. H. M. Saiful Islam specializes in complex system design, engineering decision analytics, and professional practice standards. With extensive academic and industrial experience, he has worked on high-impact engineering projects and accreditation frameworks.
